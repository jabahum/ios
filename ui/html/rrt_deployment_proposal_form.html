Proposal Guidelines
Proposal Requirements
  • Select appropriate outbreak and timeframe
  • Define clear deployment purpose and objectives
  • Propose qualified team members with specific roles
  • Include essential members and alternatives
  • Provide detailed justification for approval
Important Notes
  • Proposals require approval before deployment
  • Essential members cannot be easily replaced
  • Include all required skills for the mission
  • Provide realistic timeframes and duration
Team Composition Tips
Team Lead: Overall coordination and leadership
Deputy Lead: Backup leadership and support
Specialists: Domain experts (medical, lab, etc.)
Support Staff: Logistics, communication, drivers
Alternatives: Backup members for key positions
