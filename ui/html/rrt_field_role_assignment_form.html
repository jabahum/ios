Assignment Guidelines
Field Role Assignment
  • Assigns additional responsibilities to team members
  • Provides flexibility in field operations
  • Helps distribute workload effectively
  • Supports skill development and leadership
Important Notes
  • Ensure team member is qualified for the role
  • Consider workload and availability
  • Communicate clearly with the team member
  • Document reason for assignment
  • Monitor performance and provide support
Common Field Roles
Team Lead: Overall coordination and decision-making
Deputy Lead: Supports team lead, acts as backup
Coordinator: Manages specific activities or areas
Data Manager: Handles data collection and reporting
Safety Officer: Ensures team safety protocols
