Patient Management Help

Help and guidance for patient management

Adding New Patients

To add a new patient:

  1. Navigate to Cases section
  2. Click "New Case" button
  3. Fill in patient demographics
  4. Select appropriate outbreak
  5. Save the case
Editing Patient Information

To edit patient information:

  1. Find the patient in the cases list
  2. Click "Edit" button
  3. Update the required information
  4. Save changes
Managing Encounters

To add encounters:

  1. Open the patient case
  2. Go to Encounters tab
  3. Click "New Encounter"
  4. Fill in encounter details
  5. Save the encounter
Viewing Reports

To view patient reports:

  1. Open the patient case
  2. Click "Report" in the menu
  3. View comprehensive patient summary
